Add it to your iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari. On newer iPhones tap ••• first, then Share.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The madrasah logo will appear on your Home Screen.

Opened this from WhatsApp? Tap the Safari compass icon first so the page opens in Safari.

Add it to your Android phone

  1. Tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  3. Tap Install or Add. The madrasah logo will appear on your Home screen.

Opened this from WhatsApp? Tap the three dots and choose Open in Chrome first.

Watch the short video

Add it to your computer

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the gold Add to Home Screen button above, or the install icon at the end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. Ezziyah Teacher will appear in your apps with the madrasah logo.

Using Safari on a Mac? Choose File, then Add to Dock.
